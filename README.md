## What I'm exploring

### CrediBridge — Fintech concept
An early-stage concept for a Bangladesh-focused alternative financial assessment platform. The idea explores how consent-based income and cash-flow evidence could help people with limited traditional credit histories present their financial circumstances more clearly.

*Concept in development; not a licensed credit bureau, lender or bank-endorsed scoring service.*

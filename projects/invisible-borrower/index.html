Illustrative model and limitations

Index = 40% income stability + 35% savings buffer + 25% inverse debt burden. Each component is a synthetic 0–100 indicator. Documentation is deliberately not used as a scoring input; it is shown for comparison only.

The 65-point threshold is an arbitrary exploration aid, not a credit approval cutoff. This is not a probability of default, regulatory credit score, verified risk model, or lending recommendation. All 500 profiles are algorithmically generated, not observed Bangladesh data. No predictive validity, fairness, fraud resilience or borrower consent has been tested.
